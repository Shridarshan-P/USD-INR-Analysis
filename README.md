# Understanding USD/INR: The Role of India's Imports & Exports

## Project Overview

This project studies movements in the USD/INR exchange rate and explores their relationship with India's international trade.

The analysis will use R programming and statistical methods to investigate how India's imports, exports and trade balance are associated with movements in the Indian rupee.

Particular attention will be given to major import components such as crude oil and gold.

## Research Questions

1. How has the USD/INR exchange rate changed over time?
2. How are India's total imports related to USD/INR?
3. How are India's total exports related to USD/INR?
4. How is India's trade deficit related to USD/INR?
5. How are crude oil and gold imports related to USD/INR?
6. Can India's trade variables together help explain movements in USD/INR?

## Methodology

The project will use R programming and statistical analysis, including:

* Data collection
* Data cleaning
* Descriptive statistics
* Time-series analysis
* Data visualization
* Correlation analysis
* Regression analysis

The analysis will examine statistical relationships between India's trade variables and the USD/INR exchange rate.

Correlation does not necessarily imply causation, so the results will be interpreted carefully.

## Limitations

USD/INR is influenced by many factors, including:

* International trade
* Capital flows
* Global interest rates
* Inflation
* Global US dollar strength
* Commodity prices
* Foreign exchange market conditions
* Market sentiment

Therefore, this project will examine selected relationships rather than claim that trade variables alone determine the USD/INR exchange rate.
